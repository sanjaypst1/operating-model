# COMPASS Information Architecture

## Design intent

Avoid a card-farm Agile brochure. COMPASS is a **system**: pillars connect through a transformation loop; every process/standard/ceremony is labelled for **scale applicability**.

## Primary navigation

1. Operating Model (Pillar 1)
2. Standards (Pillar 2)
3. Coaching (Pillar 3)
4. Scale
5. Roles
6. Tools (Decision, Front-door, Maturity)
7. Research

## Compass metaphor (used lightly)

| Direction | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| North | Strategic direction & outcomes |
| East | Delivery flow & working standards |
| South | Learning, coaching & capability |
| West | Governance, assurance & improvement |
| Centre | Customer & colleague value |

## Scaling architecture

| Level | Audience | Structure |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| 1 Foundation | ~10 | One team |
| 2 Multi-team | ~30–60 | 3–6 teams |
| 3 Value stream | ~100–200 | Product leadership |
| 4 Portfolio | ~300–450 | Portfolio prioritisation |
| 5 Enterprise | ~600–700 | Federated model |

**Rule:** Do not apply enterprise governance to a foundation team. Scale through guardrails, reusable standards, and capability—not more approvals.

## Content ownership (model governance)

- Operating-model owner
- Pillar owners
- Standard owners
- Feedback → proposal → pilot → release → train → monitor → retire
